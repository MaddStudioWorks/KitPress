01

The Kingdom

A beautiful world that feels strangely wrong.

A mysterious child is entrusted with finding a missing prince. Accompanied by the Eye, explore the Royal Forest, the Mushroom World and the Forbidden Cave.

SelkoMadd combines hand-drawn 2D artwork with 3D level geometry, layered parallax and carefully crafted lighting to create a side-scrolling world with depth.

~40 min demo 2 creators 700+ assets drawn

02

Risk / Reward

Your life is your key.

Trade part of your health for magical blocks. The cost makes the journey harder, but opens otherwise unreachable places - and gives you more opportunities to uncover the kingdom's hidden lore.

03

Rhythm & Bosses

Boss encounters built around music.

SelkoMadd's boss encounters mix rhythm, timing and relentless motion. Music is not decoration: it drives the identity and intensity of each confrontation.

♪

More than ten original tracks in the demo. The full game explores jazz, metalcore, techno, classical and more - composed in-house by the team.

Three.js + WebGPU

A custom game technology built with web technologies.

MaddStudio develops its own gameplay architecture, physics, entities, shaders, lighting, save system, audio systems and more on top of Three.js and WebGPU.

“We want to show that web technologies can be used to create rich, ambitious and expressive games.”
